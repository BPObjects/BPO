What the configurator does

From a component to a whole building

A single object or an entire building: everything is set live, and leaves in the right format.

🧩

Parametric objects

Walls, slabs, columns, beams, railings, stairs, furniture, lighting… set to your dimensions.

🏢

Complete building

Storeys, column grids, façades, partitions, floor openings and layers to hide families.

BIM export

IFC (ArchiCAD / Revit), DAE (SketchUp) and universal OBJ — native elements, at real units.

RAL colours, textures, or your own images. Linings, insulation, glazing, curtain walls.

Page layout

A4→A0 sheets, scaled views, dimensions, customisable title block and PDF printing.
